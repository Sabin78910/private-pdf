Private PDF does everyday PDF jobs **on your phone**. Your documents are never uploaded: every tool works offline, with no ads and no tracking.

📷 **Scanner**: photograph forms, certificates and IDs. Edges are found automatically, the page is straightened, and filters clean up shadows. Multipage scans become one PDF.
🔤 **OCR in Nepali + English**: copy text from photos and scans, or make a scanned PDF searchable. Works without internet.
🗜️ **Compress PDF**: shrink large scans so they go through email and WhatsApp. Shows exactly how much was saved.
➕ **Merge** forms, certificates and photos into one file
✂️ **Split / extract** only the pages you need
🗂️ **Organize pages**: reorder, rotate, delete, duplicate or add blank pages
✍️ **Fill & Sign**: fill form fields, add your name and date, draw your signature with your finger
🖼️ **PDF to image**: share pages as JPG or PNG
🕘 **History** of what you did (file names only, kept on your phone)

🔒 **Privacy first**
• Files are processed on your device and never sent to any server
• Your original file is never changed: results are saved as new files
• No ads, no analytics, no account needed
• Optional Google sign-in, used only to show your name
